NewsRoom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

NewsRoom is a WhatsApp bot that sends weather, sports, and news updates to people who message it. This policy explains what information NewsRoom receives when you use it, how that information is used, and how you can ask for it to be deleted.

Information we receive

When you send a message to NewsRoom on WhatsApp, the bot receives:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • The content of the messages you send to the bot
  • Delivery information from WhatsApp, such as whether a message was sent, delivered, or read

NewsRoom does not ask for or collect any other personal information.

How we use your information

Your information is used only to:

  • Understand your request (for example, which city's weather you want)
  • Send you the weather, sports, or news update you asked for
  • Keep the service running and fix problems

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it.

Who your information is shared with

  • Meta / WhatsApp: messages are sent and received through the WhatsApp Business Platform, which is operated by Meta. Meta handles that data under WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.
  • Data providers: to answer your request, NewsRoom may send the topic of your request (such as a city name or a sports team) to third-party weather, sports, or news services. Your phone number is not sent to these services.

We do not share your information with anyone else, unless we are required to by law.

How long we keep your information

NewsRoom does not build a profile of you. Messages are processed to send your reply, and may appear in server logs for a short time so we can find and fix problems. Those logs are not used for any other purpose.

Deleting your data

You can stop using NewsRoom at any time by no longer messaging it or by blocking its number in WhatsApp. To ask for any information we hold about you to be deleted, email us at the address below with the phone number you used. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm once it is done.

Children

NewsRoom is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use WhatsApp in their country.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
